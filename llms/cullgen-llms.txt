# Cullgen

> Cullgen Inc. is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in San Diego, California, developing
> first-in-class targeted protein degradation medicines on its uSMITE (ubiquitin-mediated, small
> molecule-induced target elimination) platform and its degrader-antibody conjugate (DAC) chemistry.
> Gyre Therapeutics completed an all-stock acquisition of Cullgen on 4 May 2026.
